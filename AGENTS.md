# multipass

Workshop for our fork of [`ndycode/codex-multi-auth`](https://github.com/ndycode/codex-multi-auth).

Read `MAINTAIN.md` completely before changing the fork, and `CONTEXT.md` for the
language. `/maintain` runs a maintenance cycle from `MAINTAIN.md`; this
repository is the whole of what that skill knows about codex-multi-auth.

The fork lives at `fork/` and its worktrees at `worktrees/`. Both are ignored:
the workshop tracks the *pin* (`fork.json`), never the fork's contents.
